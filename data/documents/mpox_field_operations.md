# Mpox Field Operations Guideline (Synthetic)

## Section 1: Purpose
This guideline describes field operations for suspected mpox cases identified during community
outreach or fixed-site clinics under the fictional GuidelineCheck Health Authority.

## Section 2: Screening
Screen individuals presenting with a new unexplained rash or lesions plus at least one of fever,
swollen lymph nodes, or recent close contact with a suspected case. Screening is not a diagnosis;
suspected cases must be referred to a clinician for confirmation. This guideline does not provide
patient-specific treatment advice.

## Section 3: Field Precautions
Field workers should use gloves and a well-fitted respirator when in direct contact with a
suspected case or their bedding/clothing. Avoid direct skin contact with lesions.

## Section 4: Vaccination Coordination
Post-exposure prophylaxis vaccination coordination for eligible high-risk contacts should follow
the regional vaccination scheduling system; this guideline covers only field screening and
precautions, not dosing.

## Section 5: Reporting
Suspected cases must be reported to the regional surveillance system within 24 hours of
screening, including exposure history and screening outcome.
