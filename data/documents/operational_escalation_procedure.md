# Operational Escalation Procedure (Synthetic)

## Section 1: Purpose
This procedure defines when and how field teams escalate an outbreak response within the
fictional GuidelineCheck Health Authority operational structure.

## Section 2: Escalation Triggers
Escalate to the Regional Response Coordinator when any of the following occur: a single site
reports more than 5 confirmed cases within 7 days, a cold-chain failure affects more than 50
doses, or field teams are unable to reach a screening site for more than 24 hours.

## Section 3: Escalation Steps
Step 1: Log the triggering event in the outbreak response tracker.
Step 2: Notify the Regional Response Coordinator by phone and follow up in writing within 2
hours.
Step 3: Await coordinator confirmation before altering the standard protocol in the field.

## Section 4: Documentation
All escalations must include site ID, trigger reason, and the responding team lead's ID.
