# Measles Field Response Advisory (Synthetic)

## Section 1: Advisory Summary
This advisory provides operational guidance for field teams responding to active measles
clusters. It complements, and does not replace, the Measles Outbreak Vaccination Protocol
Version 2.0.

## Section 2: Case Isolation Guidance
Suspected cases should be isolated from communal areas for 4 days after rash onset. Field teams
should coordinate with the regional health authority before establishing a temporary isolation
area at a shelter or transport hub.

## Section 3: Contact Tracing
Identify all contacts within a 2-day window prior to rash onset. Contacts without documented
immunity should be offered the supplemental vaccination dose described in the current
Vaccination Protocol (Version 2.0) as soon as possible, ideally within 72 hours of exposure.

## Section 4: Team Safety
Field personnel entering isolation areas should use standard droplet and airborne precautions
consistent with the Respiratory Outbreak PPE Procedure in effect at the time of response.

## Section 5: Escalation
Any cluster exceeding 5 confirmed cases in a single site within 7 days should be escalated using
the Operational Escalation Procedure.
