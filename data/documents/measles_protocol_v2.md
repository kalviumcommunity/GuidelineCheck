# Measles Outbreak Vaccination Protocol (Synthetic)

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This synthetic protocol defines the recommended vaccination response for field teams operating
in a declared measles outbreak zone. It applies to mobile vaccination units, regional clinics,
and outbreak response coordinators operating under the fictional GuidelineCheck Health Authority.

## Section 2: Target Population
The standard outbreak response schedule targets children aged 6 months to 15 years within the
declared outbreak radius. Infants aged 6-11 months should receive an early supplemental dose
that does not replace the routine 12-15 month dose. Adults with uncertain vaccination history
who work in high-contact roles (shelters, transport hubs, clinics) are also eligible for a
single supplemental dose under this protocol.

## Section 3: Dosing and Administration
Field teams should administer a single 0.5 mL subcutaneous dose of the synthetic MMR-analog
vaccine used in this simulated dataset. A second dose is required at least 28 days later for
individuals who received the early supplemental dose. Cold-chain integrity must be verified
and logged before every administration session.

## Section 4: Contraindications
Do not administer to individuals with a documented severe allergic reaction to a previous dose
or vaccine component, individuals with severe immunodeficiency, or pregnant individuals. Refer
these cases to a supervising clinician; this document does not provide patient-specific medical
advice.

## Section 5: Version Notes
This is Version 2.0 of the Measles Outbreak Vaccination Protocol. It supersedes Version 1.0 in
full. Key changes from Version 1.0: the eligible age floor for the early supplemental dose was
lowered from 9 months to 6 months, and the high-contact adult category was added. Field teams
should discard printed copies of Version 1.0 and use only Version 2.0 guidance going forward.

## Section 6: Reporting
All administered doses must be logged in the outbreak response tracker within 24 hours,
including lot number, site location, and administering team ID.
