# Measles Outbreak Vaccination Protocol (Synthetic) - Version 1.0

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This synthetic protocol (Version 1.0) defined the original recommended vaccination response for
field teams operating in a declared measles outbreak zone under the fictional GuidelineCheck
Health Authority. This document has been superseded by Version 2.0 and is retained only for
historical reference and version-comparison purposes.

## Section 2: Target Population
The original schedule targeted children aged 9 months to 15 years within the declared outbreak
radius. Infants aged 9-11 months could receive an early supplemental dose. There was no
high-contact adult category in this version.

## Section 3: Dosing and Administration
Field teams administered a single 0.5 mL subcutaneous dose of the synthetic MMR-analog vaccine.
A second dose was required at least 28 days later for individuals who received the early
supplemental dose.

## Section 4: Contraindications
Do not administer to individuals with a documented severe allergic reaction to a previous dose
or vaccine component, individuals with severe immunodeficiency, or pregnant individuals.
